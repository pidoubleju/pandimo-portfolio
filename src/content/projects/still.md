## A little room to breathe

Still is a fictional wellbeing studio. This sample project explores how an identity
can feel calm and welcoming without losing its character.

## The approach

A soft palette, expressive typography, and a simple repeating wave give the studio
a visual language that works across print and digital touchpoints. The goal was
to make every detail feel intentional, with enough space for the content to lead.

- A flexible wordmark and graphic language
- A considered colour and type palette
- Stationery and simple brand applications
