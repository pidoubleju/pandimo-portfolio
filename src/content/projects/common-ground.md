## Finding a sense of belonging

Common ground is a fictional community platform. This sample explores a simple
question: how can a digital space make it easier to connect in the real world?

## From idea to experience

The concept centres on clear navigation, readable content, and a friendly visual
language. Events and shared interests become the starting points for new
connections.

- An accessible information hierarchy
- Responsive layouts for small and large screens
- A warm, consistent set of interface elements
