## Less scrolling, more living

Offline is a fictional independent publication about everyday creativity. This
sample project brings together bold typography, playful shapes, and a little
encouragement to step away from the screen.

## Making space for play

A compact visual system gives each issue room to explore a new theme. The graphic
language is deliberately simple, so it can move between a printed cover, a poster,
and a small digital announcement.
