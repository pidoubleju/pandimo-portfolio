# Mona portfolio

Mona Schmidt's portfolio presents her introduction and work across four areas of
expertise.

## Language

**Front page**:
The portfolio's main page, providing an introduction, orientation, and navigation
to the four areas of expertise.
_Avoid_: Dashboard

**Expertise area**:
One of the four themes used to present Mona's work: Brand guidelines, Merch &
brand experience, Paid & organic content, or AI-assisted imagery.
_Avoid_: Project, case study

**Expertise page**:
A dedicated detail page presenting Mona's work within one expertise area.
_Avoid_: Project page
